More than one angle is correct.

  • θp1 or θp2 ± 180° describes the same plane, so it is equally correct. (θp1 and θp2 are always 90° apart.)
  • θs ± 180° also gives +τmax. θs ± 90° is also a plane of maximum shear, but the shear on it is −τmax. Both planes carry normal stress σavg.
